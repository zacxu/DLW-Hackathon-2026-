FAQs

                
                

                    

                        What files are supported for inference?
                        
Images: jpg, jpeg, png, bmp, tif, tiff, webp. Videos: avi, mp4, mov, mkv, wmv, webm, m4v.

                    

                    

                        How should I set the emergency threshold?
                        
Start around 0.60 and tune by your false-positive tolerance. Lower catches more events but increases noise.

                    

                    

                        Can I call emergency services without running inference?
                        
Yes. Use the Standalone Emergency Contact section to generate a manual incident summary and call link.

                    

                    

                        Does the system auto-dial emergency services?
                        
No. It provides a call link and structured summary; a human operator initiates the call.
